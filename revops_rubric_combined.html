How to use this rubric

Performance reviews
Rate each relevant subcategory. Add notes with specific examples. Use per-category next steps to give clear direction, then summarize at the bottom.
Promotion decisions
Evaluate at the next level. If someone is being considered for L5, rate against the L5 rubric. Consistent across most categories — with some Exceptional — is the signal.
Hiring calibration
Use in debrief conversations after interviews. Rate what you observed against the target level. Gaps become onboarding focus areas.

How to use this rubric

Performance reviews
Rate each relevant subcategory. Management impact is evaluated through team outcomes — not just the manager's own work.
Promotion decisions
For M1 → M2, look for someone who has moved beyond dual contribution into genuine team leadership. For M2 → M3, the signal is domain ownership, not just reliable delivery.
Hiring calibration
Pay attention to people development and performance management — hardest to train, most predictive of management success.
